FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in a standard clean?
Dusting, vacuuming and mopping throughout, full kitchen and bathroom cleaning and disinfecting, beds made, trash emptied, baseboards, windowsills, mirrors and fingerprints on woodwork and switch plates. Extras like oven interiors, inside the fridge and bathroom deep scrubs are available as add-ons.
How much does it cost to clean my home?
We use flat rates by home size instead of an hourly meter, so you know your price upfront. Cleanings start at $150 for a studio or 1-bedroom and $230 for a 3-bedroom. See the full table on our Services & Pricing page.
Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?
No. We bring everything — vacuums, mops, microfiber cloths and eco-friendly products that are safe around kids and pets. You don't need to prepare anything.
Can I book a same-day cleaning?
Yes, same-day service is often available. Call or text 442.588.0028 and we'll tell you right away what we have open today.
Do I have to be home during the cleaning?
Not at all. Many clients give us access instructions and come home to a finished house. Whatever you're comfortable with works for us.
How do I pay?
You can pay securely online by card through our Pay Online page, or settle up in person after the cleaning. A small deposit holds your spot on the schedule.
