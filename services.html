Our Checklist

What's Included Every Visit

The same detailed checklist runs on every cleaning — nothing skipped.

🚿 Bathrooms

  • Sinks cleaned, disinfected and rinsed
  • Tub, shower and tiles scrubbed and disinfected
  • Chrome fixtures cleaned and shined
  • Toilets disinfected inside, out, base and behind
  • Vanity tops cleared, cleaned and reset
  • Mirrors, windowsills and baseboards
  • Floors vacuumed and washed, trash emptied

🍽️ Kitchen

  • Counters and backsplash (items removed & replaced)
  • Cabinet fronts washed or dusted
  • Sinks scrubbed and disinfected
  • Appliance exteriors: oven, microwave, fridge, dishwasher, stovetop
  • Small appliances wiped down
  • Table and chairs washed or dusted
  • Floors vacuumed and washed, trash emptied

🛏️ Bedrooms

  • Carpets vacuumed, including under beds
  • Beds made, linens changed if left out
  • Full dusting: tops, fronts and underneath
  • Picture frames, mirrors and lampshades
  • Windowsills and baseboards
  • Fingerprints off woodwork and switch plates
  • Trash emptied, light straightening

🛋️ Living & Other Areas

  • All surfaces dusted top, front and underneath
  • Upholstered furniture vacuumed
  • Cushions and pillows fluffed and straightened
  • Hardwood and tile damp-mopped
  • Carpets vacuumed
  • Baseboards dusted, cobwebs removed
  • Glass tables, picture frames and lampshades
