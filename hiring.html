Job FAQs

Questions From Applicants

Do I need my own cleaning supplies?
No. We provide all products, vacuums and equipment. You just need reliable transportation to get to jobs.
How many hours can I get?
We guarantee a weekly minimum once you're on a route, and full-time cleaners typically run about 32 billable hours plus paid drive time. Tell us the number you need and we'll tell you honestly whether we can hit it in your region before you accept anything.
Do I need experience?
Not required. We'll walk you through our checklist and you'll ride along on your first job. What matters most is showing up on time and caring about the finished result.
How and when do I get paid?
$30 an hour if you bring your own supplies from our approved list, or $25 an hour if we supply everything — you choose when you apply. Either way it's billable cleaning time plus paid drive time between jobs, paid out every Friday. At a full schedule that's roughly $960 or $800 a week gross. We'll walk through the exact numbers on our first call.
Is there a background check?
Yes. Our clients trust us in their homes, so every cleaner is vetted. It's quick and we cover the cost.
