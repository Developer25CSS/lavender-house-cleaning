How Statewide Works

Local Crews, One Standard

We don't drive a single van 200 miles. Each region has its own crew who live there — so your cleaner knows your neighborhood, and the drive time doesn't land on your bill.

  • Front Range metros: same-day and next-day slots most weeks
  • Mountain towns: same-day AirBnB turnovers between check-out and check-in
  • Western Slope and rural: scheduled route days, book 2–3 days ahead
  • Same flat pricing statewide — no surcharge for metro addresses
  • Every crew paid $27–$30/hr, so the person cleaning your home wants to keep the job

Regional FAQs

Questions About Coverage

Do you charge extra for driving to my town?
Not in any metro or Front Range area — the flat rate on our pricing page is the rate. For remote mountain or Western Slope addresses more than about 30 minutes off our route, we may add a small travel fee, and we always tell you that number before you book, never after.
Can you do a same-day clean in Denver or Colorado Springs?
Usually yes. Those two metros plus Aurora, Parker, Castle Rock and Longmont have the deepest crew coverage. Call or text and we'll tell you what's actually open today rather than promising a slot we don't have.
I own a rental in Breckenridge or Steamboat. Can you handle turnovers?
Yes — mountain turnovers are a big part of our resort work. Give us your check-out and check-in times and we'll fit the clean between them, including linens, restock and a guest-ready staging pass.
My town isn't on the list. Am I out of luck?
Not necessarily. The list covers where we run regular routes, but Colorado has a lot of small towns. Call or text your address and we'll tell you straight away whether we can reach you and how soon.
Is the price different in Aspen than in Pueblo?
No. Same flat rates by home size everywhere in the state. What changes is availability — dense metros have more open slots than rural routes.
